Lifestyle Diagnostics™ Framework

The core of the funnel. You don't sell property.
You diagnose life situations.

  • 👤
    Life Stage Where they are in life (career, family, retirement, etc.)
  • 😣
    Pain Points What's not working in their current situation?
  • 🎯
    Goals & Aspirations What do they want more of in life?
  • ⛓️
    Constraints Budget, timing, location, flexibility
  • ✨
    Ideal Outcome What does success look like for them?
You connect the dots and show them the smarter move.
