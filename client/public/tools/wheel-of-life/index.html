TheWheel of Life

Eight parts of how a week actually feels, scored from 1 to 10. The shape is usually more honest than any one number.

01Eight areasAbout ten minutes
Begin

Most people can describe a building faster than they can describe their week.

Score each area from 1 to 10. One is neglected. Ten is about as good as you'd honestly want it. First instinct tends to beat a long debate.

The wheel stays with you at the foot of the page. Adjust from there, or in each chapter. At the end I'll name the lowest areas, read six quieter needs, and offer a week, a month, and a season of small moves to bring the shape back toward round.
