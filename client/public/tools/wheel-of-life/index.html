Most people can describe a building faster than they can describe their week.

Score each area from 1 to 10. One is neglected. Ten is about as good as you'd honestly want it. First instinct tends to beat a long debate.

The wheel stays with you at the foot of the page. Adjust from there, or in each chapter. At the end I'll name the lowest areas and offer a week, a month, and a season of small moves to bring the shape back toward round.
