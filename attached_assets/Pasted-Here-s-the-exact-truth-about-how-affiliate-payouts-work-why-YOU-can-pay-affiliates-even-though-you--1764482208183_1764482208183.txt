⭐ THE CORE MISUNDERSTANDING

You’re thinking:

“I’m already an affiliate earning a commission from lenders…

✔ You’re not paying them with YOUR money.

✔ You’re splitting the affiliate commission YOU earn.

That’s it.

You’re basically acting as a sub-affiliate network.

⭐ ARE THERE ANY RESTRICTIONS OR LEGAL ISSUES?

No — except:

✔ You must always disclose affiliate relationships

(e.g., “We may earn a commission…”)

✔ Your affiliates must disclose as well

(FTC requirement)

✔ You must not pretend to be offering the loan yourself

(You are a “referral” or “comparison” engine)

✔ For consumer mortgages (NMLS), you cannot share the commission

—but you are NOT sharing mortgage commissions.
You’re sharing affiliate payouts for non-mortgage products.

So you’re totally safe.
