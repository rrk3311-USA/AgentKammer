# Agent Kammer Pre-Ad Traffic Plan

## Objective

Get the first authentic inquiries by using narrow, high-intent paths before broad paid ads.

The site should not compete for generic "NYC realtor" first. It should compete for people with a trigger, uncertainty, and money/time pressure.

## Best Near-Term Inbound Bets

### 1. Executive Relocation

Landing page:
https://www.agentkammer.com/services/executive-relocation-nyc

Why it can convert:
Executives have urgency, budget, and limited time. They need a recommendation before a broad search.

Organic angles:

- "Should I rent or buy after relocating to Manhattan?"
- "Best Manhattan neighborhoods for Midtown commute"
- "Executive relocation NYC housing strategy"

Referral partners:

- corporate relocation consultants
- executive recruiters
- private bankers
- employment attorneys
- HR leaders

## Landing Page CTA Pattern

Every page should end with:

Begin a Housing Strategy Session.

Minimum filters:

- What changed?
- Decision on the table
- Timeline
- Budget/readiness
- Best next step

This keeps the lead qualified without turning the site into a long form.
