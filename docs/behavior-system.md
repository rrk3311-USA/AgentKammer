# Agent Kammer Behavior System

The next version of Agent Kammer should be designed around behaviors, not screens. The interface should feel like a living AI workspace where the page, Decision Blueprint, and assistant respond continuously to visitor context.

## Core Behaviors

### Behavior 001: Page Awareness

The Decision Companion always knows the current route, page title, topics, prerequisites, and related next steps.

### Behavior 002: Proactive Guidance

The Decision Companion may point out the most useful next uncertainty instead of waiting for a button click.

### Behavior 003: Automatic Blueprint Growth

The Decision Blueprint updates after each route visit, message, profile signal, and recommendation.

### Behavior 004: Persistent Conversation State

Conversation, lead score, visitor state, industry lens, visited pages, and known profile signals persist across routes.

### Behavior 005: Structured Page Metadata

Every page exposes structured metadata to the knowledge graph: topics, prerequisites, related routes, and recommended next actions.

### Behavior 006: Real-Time Personalization

Pages may adapt visible modules based on known profile signals such as children, pets, commute, industry, risk tolerance, and timeline.

### Behavior 007: Explain Recommendations

The Decision Companion explains why it recommends a page, section, or next question.

### Behavior 008: Interruptible Journey

The visitor can browse, scroll, switch topics, answer, ignore, or resume without losing context.

### Behavior 009: Guided Navigation

When confidence is high, the Decision Companion may offer to open, scroll to, or highlight the next relevant page or section.

### Behavior 010: Living Cards

Cards can change labels based on profile signals, such as "New because you mentioned staff quality" or "Relevant because timeline is still unknown."

## Product Principle

Agent Kammer is not a real estate website with an AI panel. It is a housing decision workspace where the Decision Companion, Blueprint, page content, and knowledge graph continuously react to what the visitor is learning.
