## 1. The frame: a practice, not a pipeline

Agent Kammer is building a **residential advisory practice** — not "looking for clients" or running a listing funnel. That distinction changes the language everywhere:

- Clients pay for **ongoing judgment**, not listing access.
- The product is an **ongoing advisory relationship**. A single paid session is a valid, complete engagement — not a failed upsell.
- **Calls are one access method** among several (session, email, written recap, portfolio review) — the practice does not center everything on "getting on a call."
- Retire "looking for clients" / "leads" / "prospects" as public-facing framing. The practice has **clients** and **Cases** (see [`DECISION-CASES.md`](./DECISION-CASES.md)).

**Relationship over calls (principle):** every surface — docs, CTAs, forms — should read as *"how does this person continue being advised"* rather than *"how do we get them on the phone."* A call is the delivery mechanism for one session; it is never the product itself.

## 2. Rename: Discovery Call → Housing Strategy Session

**Retired (public):** "Discovery Call" — reads like a sales-qualification call, not a paid deliverable.

**Canonical primary name:**

> **Housing Strategy Session**

This is the canonical name for the first paid session in every public surface (site copy, forms, CTAs, docs written for clients).

**Approved aliases** (use in docs / internal variety; don't rotate all of them on the same page):

| Alias | When to use |
|---|---|
| Residential Strategy Consultation | Slightly more formal / B2B-adjacent framing (e.g. executive relocation, corporate HR contact) |
| Decision Advisory Session | When emphasizing the decision-support nature over the "strategy" word |
| Portfolio Strategy Review | When the session is really the Residential Portfolio Review offer (multiple properties) |
| Housing Clarity Session | Softer, more emotional framing for life-change-first audiences |

**Internal only — do not use publicly:** "Discovery Call," "sales call," "qualification call," "intro call."

**What changes vs. the old model:** a Housing Strategy Session is not a free pre-qualification call before the "real" paid work starts. It **is** the paid work — a defined ~60-minute session with a defined written deliverable (action summary). Nothing about the underlying auth, CRM, or Decision Blueprint mechanics needs to change; only the public name and the fact that it is presented as a paid deliverable, not a free gate.

## 3. Offer stack

Document as **phase pricing** — ranges, not fixed published prices, until the owner locks final numbers. Nothing here should be wired to a payments system; this is a pricing/offer reference for copy and internal ops, not a checkout flow.

### À la carte

| Offer | Format | What it resolves | Price band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Housing Strategy Session** | ~60 min | One major decision: buy vs. wait, stay vs. move, building comparison, renovate vs. relocate, executive relocation. Includes a written action summary. | $250–500 |
| **Building Second Opinion** | Session + written notes | One specific building or property: strengths, risks, resale, lifestyle fit, questions to investigate before committing | *(price TBD by owner — align to Housing Strategy Session band)* |
| **Residential Portfolio Review** | Session + written notes | All properties a client owns: keep / sell / renovate / rent, lifestyle fit, long-term goals | *(price TBD by owner — typically above single-session band given scope)* |

### Memberships (ongoing guidance — not "a bundle of meetings")

Memberships are the practice's continuity layer. Frame them as **standing advisory relationships**, not punch-cards of calls.

| Tier | Price band | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | $250–500/mo | Monthly strategy touchpoint · email questions · annual portfolio review · market updates · decision summaries |
| **Executive Advisory** | $750–1,500/mo | Two monthly sessions · priority scheduling · portfolio oversight · building research · renovation guidance · vendor recommendations · family planning discussions |
| **Private Residential Office** | $2,500–5,000+/mo | Family office for residential real estate: reasonable unlimited strategy sessions · coordination with architects, inspectors, lenders, attorneys, brokers · portfolio + relocation + acquisition strategy · ongoing oversight · annual residential master plan |

**Writing rule:** when describing a membership, lead with what the client *has ongoing access to* (judgment, oversight, coordination), and mention session counts as a supporting detail — not the headline.

- Weak: "Get 2 calls a month for $750."
- Strong: "Ongoing portfolio oversight and two monthly strategy sessions, so decisions get made before they become urgent."

## 4. Funnel

```txt
1. Visitor reads insights / Research Library / Belonging Assessment
2. Books a paid Housing Strategy Session
3. Receives a written strategy summary + action plan
4. Soft next step (no pressure):
     - Some clients need exactly one conversation. That is a complete, successful engagement.
     - Others prefer ongoing advisory via a membership as decisions keep evolving.
```

**Do not** treat step 4 as a hard close. The practice's judgment includes knowing when "one Housing Strategy Session and nothing more" is the correct, complete outcome for a client — the same way "do nothing" can be the correct real estate recommendation (see Decision Philosophy in the brand manual).

**Relationship to the existing decision funnel:** the Belonging Assessment / Decision Profile front door is unchanged. The Housing Strategy Session **is** the paid session previously named "Discovery Call" in that funnel:

```txt
Belonging Assessment → Decision Profile → Housing Strategy Session (paid) → Decision Blueprint™ → Curated local expert (when a transaction is right)
```

The **Decision Blueprint™** remains the written deliverable name for the artifact produced after a session (see [`DECISION-BLUEPRINT.md`](./DECISION-BLUEPRINT.md)). For à la carte copy aimed at a general residential-advisory audience (not the NYC decision-hub product), "written action summary" or "written strategy summary" are acceptable plain-language equivalents of the same artifact.
